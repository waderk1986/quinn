About the Candidate

A Sheriff Who Knows This County

Quinn Archibeque isn't a politician. He's a neighbor, a public servant, and a man who has dedicated his career to keeping Delta County safe.

Quinn has chosen to make Delta County his own — it's where he has raised his family, planted his roots, and built a life he's proud of. He understands the values and challenges of our community not from a briefing, but from a lifetime of living them. With nearly 28 years in law enforcement across the Cedaredge Police Department, Delta Police Department, and the Delta County Sheriff's Office, his commitment to this community runs deep. This May marks 18 years at the Sheriff's Office alone — rising from volunteer deputy all the way to Undersheriff. His vision for the Sheriff's Office is built on the same foundation as the community he serves: integrity, accountability, and genuine care for every resident.

Track Record

A Life of Service

Early Career

Law Enforcement Foundation

Began his career in public safety serving the communities of Delta County — including the Cedaredge Police Department and Delta Police Department — building the skills, judgment, and relationships that would define decades of dedicated service.

Sheriff's Office — 18 Years

Rising Through the Ranks

Joined the Delta County Sheriff's Office as a volunteer deputy and rose through every level of the department: Patrol Deputy, Detention Officer, Detective, Detective Sergeant, and now Undersheriff — earning the trust of colleagues and community members through consistent performance and uncompromising integrity.

Today

Ready to Lead as Sheriff

With nearly 28 years of experience and deep roots in Delta County, Quinn is ready to step into the role of Sheriff — bringing the full depth of his service record to lead the department into a stronger, safer future for every resident.
